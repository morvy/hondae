Privacy Policy

Honda e Dashboard (Android, package dev.moped.hondae) · Effective 1 October 2026

  • No account, no ads, no analytics, no crash reporting, no tracking.
  • The developer does not collect, receive or store any of your data. There is no developer server.
  • Everything the app reads and records stays on your phone, in storage you control.
  • The only data that leaves the phone is optional ABRP telemetry, sent only if you switch it on with your own ABRP token, or files you choose to share yourself.

Who we are

Honda e Dashboard is an independent app made by a single developer. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or connected to Honda Motor Co., Ltd. Questions about this policy: moped@jepan.sk.

Where your data is stored

While a drive or charge is being recorded, its file is kept in the app's private storage. When it is finished, it is moved to a folder you choose in Settings → File storage. Drives are GPX files; charging sessions are CSV files. You can open, move or delete these files at any time, inside the app or with any file manager.

Settings backups are only created when you export them yourself, to a file and location you pick.

If Android's own backup is turned on for your Google account, Android may include the app's settings in that backup, as it does for most apps. That backup is handled by Google under your account's settings, not by the developer, and you can turn it off in Android's settings.

A Better Route Planner (ABRP) telemetry — optional, off by default

If you turn on ABRP telemetry and enter your own ABRP token, the app sends live data to ABRP's service (api.iternio.com) over an encrypted HTTPS connection, about every 5 seconds while connected to the car. This lets ABRP plan routes from the real state of your battery.

What is sent: state of charge and health, power, speed, location (latitude, longitude, heading, elevation), charging or driving state, pack voltage and current, battery and outside temperature, odometer, battery capacity, and a timestamp. Your ABRP token identifies your ABRP vehicle.

Once received, this data is governed by ABRP's own privacy policy, available on abetterrouteplanner.com. Switch the feature off in the app's settings to stop sending immediately.

Sharing

The app does not share, sell or transfer your data to anyone. A log file only leaves your phone when you tap Share and pick where it goes using Android's share sheet; what happens then depends on the app you choose.

Android Auto

When your phone is connected to Android Auto, the app shows its values on the car's screen. This display goes from your phone to your car only.

Retention and deletion

Data stays on your phone until you delete it. Delete individual drives or charges in the app, delete the files from your chosen folder, or clear the app's storage. Uninstalling the app removes its private storage and settings; files in the folder you chose stay there until you delete them. Since the developer holds no data about you, there is nothing to request or delete on our side.

Security

Data in the app's private storage is protected by Android's app sandbox. The ABRP connection uses HTTPS. The app only reads from the car; it never writes to it or changes any of its settings.

Children

The app is meant for drivers of a Honda e and is not directed at children. It does not knowingly collect any data from anyone, children included.

Changes to this policy

If the app's handling of data changes, this page will be updated and the effective date above changed before the new version of the app is released.
